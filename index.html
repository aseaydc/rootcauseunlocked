Is the quiz a medical diagnosis?

No. The quiz is for education and screening. It can help you organize questions for a qualified healthcare professional, but it cannot establish a diagnosis or determine treatment.

Where should I start?

Start with the free symptom quiz, or browse the resource library if you would prefer to read first. You can learn about discovery sessions whenever you are ready.

Can I keep working with my current clinicians?

Yes. These resources are intended to support informed conversations with your healthcare team. For prescribed medications, keep your prescriber in the loop.

Who is behind Root Cause Unlocked?

Root Cause Unlocked is an educational offering from Root Health NC / Seay Wellness, led by Adam Seay, D.C., a chiropractor in North Carolina.
