Does this sound familiar

You have done everything you were told to do.

  • You have seen more than one provider, and each looked at one piece.
  • Your bloodwork was called normal, but nobody explained what normal meant for you.
  • You are on a medication that helps, and you still do not feel like yourself.
  • Your symptoms come and go in a pattern nobody has connected.
  • You have been handed a diagnosis that names the symptom without explaining the cause.
  • You have tried elimination diets and supplements without knowing what you were solving for.

Being treated and being well are not the same thing. Appropriate care can be in place and real questions can still be unanswered. Both things are true at once, and holding both is the beginning of getting somewhere.

Who is behind this

Adam Seay, D.C.

Root Cause Unlocked is the educational arm of Root Health NC, a practice built around a specific frustration: people arriving after years of care with no one having put the whole picture together.

The work here is functional and structural, and it is honest about its limits. A chiropractic physician does not replace your primary care provider, your endocrinologist, or your rheumatologist. What this practice does is look across systems, take your history seriously, and help you arrive at your next appointment with better questions than you left the last one with.

Everything on this site is educational. It does not diagnose, and it is not a substitute for care from your own clinicians. If you take a prescribed medication, keep taking it and keep your prescriber in the loop.
