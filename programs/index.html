What they share

Every program works the same way

The condition changes. The method does not.

  • Your history is the primary document. Most of what matters is already in your story, in an order nobody has laid out yet.
  • Testing answers a question. If a test would not change what happens next, it does not get ordered.
  • Your other clinicians stay involved. This works alongside your physician and your specialists, never around them.
  • You learn the reasoning. You should be able to explain your own case to the next provider you see.
  • Honest limits. When something falls outside what this practice should handle, you get told that plainly.

Not sure which one applies? That is the normal starting position, and it is exactly what the free assessment is for. It looks across systems rather than assuming you have already picked the right box.
