Recognition

Does this sound like you

  • Pain that is widespread rather than located anywhere in particular, and that moves, so no single joint or injury ever quite explains it.
  • Sleep that never restores. You are in bed the right number of hours and wake up as though you were not.
  • A fatigue that is not sleepiness, and a crash after exertion that arrives a day late and lasts longer than the activity that caused it.
  • Brain fog that makes words and names go missing mid-sentence, which is frightening in a way the pain is not.
  • Gut symptoms, headaches, sensitivity to noise, light, cold, or smells, all filed as separate problems by separate people.
  • A stack of normal results, a diagnosis of exclusion, and a prescription, with no explanation of what was ruled out or what to look at next.

One safety note, before you buy

Several supplements commonly recommended for fibromyalgia act on serotonin. This population is unusually likely to already be taking something that does too, including duloxetine, milnacipran, amitriptyline, an SSRI, or tramadol. Combining them carries a real risk, and the supplement guide leads with that rather than burying it. If you take any of those, read the interaction section first and bring the actual ingredient label to your prescriber, not the product name.
