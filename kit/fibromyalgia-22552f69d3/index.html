Before you start anything

The supplement guide contains medication interaction warnings that matter a great deal in fibromyalgia, particularly if you take an antidepressant, duloxetine, milnacipran, or tramadol. Please read that section before you buy or take anything, and bring the actual ingredient label to your prescriber rather than the product name.

When you are ready to test

Ordering the tests in this guide

The lab guide names the tests worth asking for and shows you how to read them once they come back. If your physician will order them, that is usually the cheaper route and worth asking for first.

If you would rather not wait, or you have already been told your labs are normal, you can order many of these yourself through my patient portal. Not every panel named in the guide is available there. A few are ordered through the office instead, and you can ask me about those.

Order your labs

Ordering a test and knowing what to do with the result are two different things. Bring your results to your physician, or to a Discovery Session, before you act on them.
